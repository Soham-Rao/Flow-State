# Profile and Personal Settings

Your profile settings control how you appear to other people and how your personal identity is represented inside the workspace.

## What belongs here

Typical profile-level information includes:

- full name
- display name
- username or account identity fields
- bio or short personal description
- age
- date of birth
- other user-facing details shown in hover cards or profile areas

## Why your profile matters

A profile is not just decoration. It helps the rest of the team:

- recognize you quickly
- understand your role or context
- know who they are mentioning or assigning work to

If your profile is vague, outdated, or confusing, the rest of the workspace becomes harder to use.

## Good profile habits

- use a recognizable display name
- keep the bio short and useful
- avoid placeholder text if the workspace is real and shared
- review your profile after major role or team changes

## What the fields usually mean

### Full name

This is the fuller personal identity stored on your account. Depending on the current screen, it may not always be the most prominent displayed field, but it still matters as part of your user record.

### Username

A username is usually the short handle associated with your account. It is especially important when the workspace uses `@username` mentions.

### Display name

Your display name is the friendly visible name people most often recognize around the app.

### Bio

Your bio is a short summary about you, your role, or what teammates should know.

### Age and date of birth

These are profile-level personal details. They are not the same as workspace permissions or admin settings.

## Saving and unsaved changes

The profile page may show:

- unsaved changes
- saving state
- saved confirmation

Do not assume that typing is the same as saving. Wait for the save action and confirmation when you change profile details.

## Hover cards and identity

FlowState may show user hover previews. These help people confirm:

- who you are
- what role you hold
- whether they are talking to or assigning the correct person

That is why profile clarity improves collaboration beyond just appearance.

## What does not belong on this page

This page is not for:

- role management
- workspace-wide permission changes
- announcements
- bug report triage
- invite administration

Those belong elsewhere in the product.
